This was Anthropic's week: new frontier models and a priced IPO dominated the news, while a leadership shock at Nvidia and new state-level regulation added to the sense of an industry in transition.

## Anthropic's models and IPO

Anthropic released its Mythos and Fable models, its first frontier releases since Dario Amodei's public call for a slowdown. Coverage framed the launch around concerns about recursive self-improvement, and the tension between that rhetoric and continued frontier shipping was the week's most discussed story. The company followed up a day later by setting an IPO price range of $70 to $78 per share, with trading reportedly expected to begin on November 19. Reports put the valuation target at $1.4 trillion, with bankers possibly aiming higher. Anthropic also launched a new suite of office productivity tools ahead of the listing, and Microsoft is now enabling Claude by default for UK Copilot tenants, a notable distribution win.

## Nvidia, Google, and OpenAI

The biggest surprise came from Nvidia, where Jensen Huang is out as CEO and CFO Colette Kress has been named interim replacement. Separately, reporting showed that Trump backed Nvidia chip controls aimed at China three times while Commerce Secretary Lutnick stalled them. Google made Gemini 4 Carbon available to developers and expanded its TPU family with the 8tx and 8i Max, aimed at agentic workloads. Google DeepMind also released a new robotics model and a next-generation gene prediction model. OpenAI introduced Businesses in ChatGPT and launched a set of security products with blunt messaging about the risks of going without them.

## Regulation and the rest

New York's AI safety law took effect, putting OpenAI and Anthropic under new state oversight. Meta introduced Muse Ember, positioned as a cost-efficient model for AI agents. OpenAI acquired Odessia, a travel startup from the founder of Sonder, continuing its run of acquisitions. Mercor raised $750 million at a $20 billion valuation to bring AI agent evaluation to enterprises. A Hugging Face executive warned that cyberattacks could push open-source AI toward more restricted releases.

To watch next week: Anthropic's planned November 19 trading debut, and how Nvidia's leadership change plays out.
